Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Escape Rush handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information We Collect

Escape Rush does not directly require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the game.

However, third-party services used by the game may automatically collect certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising performance information.

Advertising

Escape Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process data in order to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Consent and Advertising Preferences

Depending on your location, you may be asked for consent before personalized advertising or certain advertising technologies are used.

Third-Party Services

The game may use third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play Services. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed through the game and its third-party services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, please contact us using the developer contact information provided on the Escape Rush Google Play Store page.
